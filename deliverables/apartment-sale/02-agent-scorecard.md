# Agent Scorecard — Saigon South Residences 72 m2 River View

## How to Use

Call 6-8 candidates, score each 0-100 using the rubric below, take the top 3 for the open panel. Score independently — do not share one agent's answers with another. Record evidence (unit numbers, dates, screenshots) in the notes column.

## Scoring Rubric (100 points total)

| # | Criterion | Weight | 0 points | Partial points | Full points |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Verified project closings in the last 12 months | 30 | No verifiable SSR closing supplied, or only listings/offers. | 1 verifiable SSR closing with block/floor and month, cross-checkable with the management board. (15 pts) | 2+ verifiable SSR closings with block/floor and month in the last 12 months, with at least one cross-checked. (30 pts) |
| 2 | Written comparative market analysis (CMA) quality | 25 | Submits portal **asking** prices instead of **signed transaction** prices as comparables, regardless of presentation quality. Scores 0 on this criterion automatically. | Submits a mix of asking and signed prices, or signed prices without dates/areas. (10 pts) | Submits 3+ **signed transaction** prices for SSR 70-76 m2 in the last 90 days with date, area, price in ty and tr/m2, and source. (25 pts) |
| 3 | Response latency | 15 | No written follow-up within 24 hours of first call. | Written follow-up within 24 hours but after 4 hours. (7 pts) | Written follow-up (Zalo/email) within 4 hours of first call. (15 pts) |
| 4 | Marketing commitment | 15 | Will not commit to paid placement or gives a vague promise. | Commits verbally to boosted batdongsan.com.vn or Nha Tot placement but will not put spend in writing. (7 pts) | Commits in writing to a specific paid placement (e.g. boosted batdongsan.com.vn listing + Nha Tot push) with stated spend. (15 pts) |
| 5 | Live buyer queue | 10 | No named buyer waiting for a 2BR at SSR right now. | Describes a buyer in general terms but cannot name budget/timeline. (5 pts) | Names at least one live buyer with budget, timeline, and requirement (e.g. 2BR river view, 5.3-5.6 ty, wants to view this week). (10 pts) |
| 6 | Terms acceptance | 5 | Pushes back on 1.5% winner-takes-all, the fixed 5.95 ty price, or the 30-day first-registration rule. | Accepts most terms but asks to negotiate one element. (2 pts) | Accepts all panel terms — 1.5% winner-takes-all + 0.3% bonus, fixed 5.95 ty price, seller-owned assets, 30-day first-registration — without argument. (5 pts) |

**Weights sum to 100.** The CMA criterion is the primary filter in the current wide bid-ask spread: an agent who cannot distinguish asking from transacted prices cannot price this unit correctly.

## Selection Rule

Rank by total score. Appoint the top 3. If scores tie for 3rd, prefer the agent with the higher CMA score, then the higher Closings score. Do not appoint an agent who scored 0 on the CMA criterion.

Verify with a licensed Vietnamese notary (phong cong chung) and tax advisor before signing.
