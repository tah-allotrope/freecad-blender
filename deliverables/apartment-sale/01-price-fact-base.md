## River View Premium

The single highest live asking price observed — **71 m2 at 5.95 ty (83.8 tr/m2)** — is explicitly a **river-view** unit (row 4 above). The subject apartment is confirmed river-view under DEC-001 and therefore prices against that comparable rather than the 72-80 tr/m2 standard-outlook band.
