**Date:** 2026-08-29

**Property:** Apartment 72.0 m2, 2 bedrooms / 2 bathrooms, Block `[[BLOCK]]`, Floor `[[FLOOR]]`, Saigon South Residences, Nguyen Huu Tho, Phuoc Kien, Nha Be, Ho Chi Minh City. River view. Fully furnished. So hong (freehold pink book) issued. No mortgage. Empty and available to show.

**Seller:** [[OWNER NAME]] — holder of the So hong.

**Panel structure:** Open panel of exactly three agents appointed simultaneously from day one. No exclusivity is granted to any agent. The seller may communicate with all three throughout.

## 1. Fixed listing price

* The listing price is **5.95 ty** (82.6 tr/m2 for 72.0 m2), fixed and identical across all three agents and across all portals and channels.
* No agent may advertise, quote, or publish any other price without prior written consent from the seller.
* The **lowest price any agent may present to a buyer is 5.35 ty** (the published floor). Any offer at or above 5.35 ty will be submitted to the seller in writing. An offer below 5.35 ty will not be presented as acceptable without the seller's explicit instruction.

## 2. Commission — winner takes all

* **Base commission: 1.5% of gross contract value**, paid to the single agent who introduces the buyer that signs the notarised sale-purchase agreement.
* **Speed bonus: an additional 0.3% of gross contract value** if the notarised sale-purchase agreement is signed within 45 calendar days of the listing going live.
* Total when the bonus is earned: 1.8% of gross contract value. No other fees are payable by the seller.
* **Losing panel agents receive nothing**, regardless of viewings conducted or offers submitted.
* Commission is payable only on completion of the notarised transfer and receipt of the contract value.

## 3. Panel discipline rules — non-negotiable conditions of appointment

All three rules below are **non-negotiable conditions** of the panel appointment. Acceptance of this terms sheet constitutes acceptance of all three.

### (a) One fixed listing price identical across all three agents

As stated in Section 1: 5.95 ty everywhere, no deviation, no agent-specific pricing.

### (b) Seller-owned marketing assets only

* The seller will issue an identical photo, video, and floorplan asset pack to all three agents (see `07-photo-shot-list.md` for the pack contents).
* **No agent may shoot, edit, or publish their own photographs, video, or floorplan of the unit.** All published material must come from the seller-owned pack.
* Agents may not crop, filter, or alter the assets beyond standard portal resizing. No agent watermark may obscure the unit.

### (c) First-registration buyer attribution

* The agent who **first registers a named buyer in writing** (full name + phone number, by email or Zalo message to the seller) **owns that buyer for 30 calendar days**.
* During those 30 days, if that buyer signs, the registering agent earns the commission even if the buyer later contacts a different panel agent.
* After 30 days with no signed deposit contract, the buyer becomes unregistered and any panel agent may re-register them; the first to re-register owns the next 30-day window.
* The seller will maintain a dated registration log and share it with the panel on request.

**Worked example — disputed buyer:**

> Day 2: Agent A registers "Nguyen Van X, 0901 234 567" in writing. The seller logs it.
> Day 10: the same buyer contacts Agent B independently and asks to view. Agent B checks with the seller, is told the buyer is registered to Agent A until Day 32, and must refer the buyer back to Agent A.
> Day 20: the buyer signs a 5% dat coc deposit contract through Agent A. Agent A is the winner. Agent B receives nothing.
> If instead no deposit is signed by Day 32, the registration lapses. On Day 33, Agent B may register the same buyer anew and would then own the buyer until Day 63.

## 4. Offer handling

* Every offer must be submitted **in writing** (email or Zalo message) stating buyer full name, offered price in ty, deposit readiness, and whether the buyer is cash or mortgage-financed.
* Cash buyers are preferred. Mortgage-financed buyers are accepted **only on presentation of a bank pre-approval letter**, and their close is capped at 45 calendar days (see Section 6).
* Deposit: **5% of contract value (dat coc)**, subject to **forfeiture on buyer default**, as the binding step before notarisation.
* Close after deposit: **30 calendar days** for cash buyers; **45 calendar days maximum** for mortgage-financed buyers.

## 5. Portal placement

* Primary Vietnamese portals: **batdongsan.com.vn** and **nhatot.com**. Each agent is expected to fund their own boosted placement as part of their marketing commitment scored in the selection process.

## 6. Mortgage-financed buyers

* Accepted only with a bank pre-approval letter.
* Close capped at 45 calendar days.
* The seller notes that a bank valuation coming in below the contract price can reopen the negotiation around week 4, which is the specific reason cash buyers are preferred.

## 7. Duration and termination

* This mandate runs from the listing-go-live date until the unit is sold or until the seller terminates it in writing.
* There is no automatic time-based price reduction. The price is held to day 90 and then reassessed against fresh comparables. The seller will consult the panel before any price change.
* The seller may remove an agent from the panel for breach of any of the three discipline rules above, with written notice.
