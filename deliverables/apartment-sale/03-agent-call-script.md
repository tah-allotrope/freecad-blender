# Agent Call Script — Saigon South Residences 72 m2 River View

Use this script in order for each of the 6-8 candidates. Record answers verbatim and score against `02-agent-scorecard.md` after the call. Allow 15-20 minutes per call.

## Before You Call

Have ready: the subject unit facts (72.0 m2, 2BR/2WC, block `[[BLOCK]]`, floor `[[FLOOR]]`, river view, fully furnished, So hong issued, no mortgage, list 5.95 ty, floor for agents 5.35 ty), the scorecard, and a stopwatch for response latency.

## Script

### 1. Verified project closings in the last 12 months (Weight 30)

> "How many Saigon South Residences units have you personally closed in the last 12 months, and can you give me two unit numbers with closing months that I can cross-check with the building management board?"

* Strong: names blocks and floors (e.g. "Block A, 12th floor, closed March 2026; Block C, 8th floor, closed June 2026") without hesitation, offers to share transfer documents with personal data redacted.
* Weak: talks about listings, viewings, or offers instead of closings; says "many" without specifics; claims confidentiality prevents naming any unit.
* Instruction: ask for two unit numbers and closing months that can be cross-checked with the building management board. Record them.

### 2. Written CMA quality (Weight 25)

> "Can you send me a written Comparative Market Analysis for 70-76 m2 units at Saigon South Residences based on signed transaction prices from the last 90 days, with date, area, price in ty and tr/m2 for each comp?"

* Strong: explains the difference between asking and transacted prices, cites 3+ recent signed deals with dates and tr/m2, notes the spread between asking and closing in the current market.
* Weak: sends a screenshot of portal asking prices as the CMA — scores 0 on this criterion regardless of how polished the document looks. Also weak: mixes asking and transacted without labelling which is which.
* Note: an agent who submits portal asking prices instead of signed transaction prices scores 0 on the CMA criterion regardless of presentation quality.

### 3. Response latency (Weight 15)

> No question to ask — measure it. Note the time of your first call/message and the time their written follow-up (Zalo, email, or message with the CMA) arrives.

* Strong: written follow-up within 4 hours.
* Weak: no written follow-up within 24 hours, or only a phone call with no written material. Predicts how they will treat your buyers.

### 4. Marketing commitment (Weight 15)

> "What paid marketing will you commit to for this listing, and will you put the placement and spend in writing? Specifically, will you run a boosted listing on batdongsan.com.vn and a push on nhatot.com?"

* Strong: names a specific product (e.g. batdongsan.com.vn VIP Diamond / VIP Gold, Nha Tot boost package), states the spend in VND, and agrees to put it in writing.
* Weak: "We will market it well on all portals" with no product name, no amount, and no written commitment.
* Note: boosted placement on batdongsan.com.vn is the scored marketing-commitment item; get the spend in writing.

### 5. Live buyer queue (Weight 10)

> "Do you have a named buyer waiting for a 2-bedroom at Saigon South Residences right now — someone with a budget and timeline you can describe?"

* Strong: describes a live buyer with budget (e.g. 5.3-5.6 ty), requirement (2BR, river view preferred), and timing ("wants to view this week"). On-site brokers often can; city-wide brands often cannot.
* Weak: "We have many buyers" with no budget, timeline, or requirement; or describes a buyer for a different project/area.

### 6. Terms acceptance (Weight 5)

> "The mandate is an open panel of three agents from day one: fixed listing price 5.95 ty identical across all three, seller-owned photos/video/floorplan only, 1.5% winner-takes-all commission plus 0.3% bonus if the notarised agreement is signed within 45 days, and a 30-day first-registration buyer attribution rule. Do you accept all of these as written?"

* Strong: accepts without argument.
* Weak: pushes back on commission, fixed price, or attribution; asks for exclusivity or a higher rate; wants to shoot their own photos.
* Record any pushback verbatim — it predicts negotiation behaviour with buyers.

## Closing Line (every call)

> "Thank you. I will send you the written mandate terms sheet. Please confirm acceptance in writing within 24 hours if you wish to be considered for the panel of three."

## After All Calls

1. Score each agent 0-100 using the rubric in `02-agent-scorecard.md`.
2. Rank and appoint the top 3. Do not appoint anyone who scored 0 on the CMA criterion.
3. Send the identical `04-mandate-terms-sheet.md` to all three appointed agents and collect signed acceptance before publishing any listing.

Verify with a licensed Vietnamese notary (phong cong chung) and tax advisor before signing.
