Contractor As Drawn

7 storeys, 528 m² on a 3.96 × 25.00 m plot

Design Intent

A seven-storey Vietnamese tube house (trệt / lửng / tầng 2–5 / sân thượng) reconstructed from the contractor's own drawing set on a 3,960 × 25,000 mm plot, with the drawn core — a 3,005 mm U-return stair, a 1,500 × 1,600 lift and a light well — repeated identically on every level.

The ground floor runs garage → living room (P.KHÁCH) → stair core → WC → combined kitchen + dining (BẾP & ĂN); the mezzanine opens a double-height void over the garage and living room (declared as a storey void, not a fake room) with the family room (P.SINH HOẠT) at the rear; floors 2–4 repeat two bedrooms (one front with ensuite and balcony, one rear); floor 5 carries the altar room (P.THỜ); the roof terrace (SÂN THƯỢNG) opens front and rear with the lift plant room (Ô KỸ THUẬT THANG MÁY) standing on the roof slab.

All departures from the drawing are ledgered in designs/contractor-as-drawn.fidelity.md: the stair shaft is enlarged to 3,005 × 4,000 mm to hold a Blondel-compliant U-return, the lift shaft is inferred (no shaft is labelled on the floor plans), and the roof glazing is represented by an open roof void over the stair rather than glass.

Requirements for the Architect

  • Confirm the inferred lift shaft (2,000 × 1,800 mm, byte-identical on all levels) against the unlabelled walled void on the drawing.
  • Confirm the enlarged stair shaft (3,005 × 4,000 mm) — the drawn 3,005 × 3,200 shaft cannot hold a U-return at the 3,800 mm storey height.
  • Confirm the reconstructed programme room-by-room against the sheets (see designs/contractor-as-drawn.measurements.md).
