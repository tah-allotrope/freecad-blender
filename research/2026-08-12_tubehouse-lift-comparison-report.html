01Executive Summary

Lead with the answer: at 5 stops in a 1000×1400 mm shaft, traction MRL is the mainstream fit and hydraulic is not the cheap option in Vietnam.

WHAT TO DO

Traction MRL from a brand with an official VN entity

KONE MonoSpace Home (machine fully inside the hoistway, 0.4–1.0 m/s, 250–630 kg, up to 6 stops) or Otis Gen2 Life (MRL, up to 45 m rise / 14 stops) — the 600–800 triệu fully-imported class, or 400–800 triệu liên doanh. Regenerative drive keeps running costs lowest.

Budget path: local-assembled traction at 260–350 triệu — acceptable only with demonstrated kiểm định (inspection) compliance and a real warranty contract.

WHAT TO SKIP

Hydraulic at 5 stops, “platform lifts”, parallel imports

Imported hydraulic (Domuslift) runs 900 triệu–1 tỷ and still needs a machine room + deeper pit — no advantage at this stop count. Screw-driven (Cibes, 800 triệu–1.2 tỷ) only if a no-pit/no-machine-room retrofit is the hard constraint.

Never accept an EN 81-41 / TCVN 6396-41 “platform lift” (≤0.15 m/s) as a passenger lift, and no parallel-imported units without local warranty (your standing rule).

03Findings Grid — Technology Verdicts

Verdict per decision criterion. “MRL” = machine-room-less traction (gearless). Evidence links inline.

Space

Traction MRL wins. Machine sits inside the hoistway (KONE MonoSpace Home; Otis Gen2 Life; Schindler 3300 “fits into the footprint of a hydraulic elevator”). Hydraulic needs a machine room (or remote power unit) + deeper pit; VN “không hố pit” marketing applies to traction/screw minis, not hydraulic.

Caution: MRL needs more headroom above the top landing — your Floor 4 has the roof terrace above it. Verify per product. kone.vn · otis.com/vi · schindler.com

Cost

Local traction wins at 5 stops. 260–350 triệu installed vs imported hydraulic at 900 triệu–1 tỷ (GDE price table). The “hydraulic is cheaper” claim holds only at very low rise / in the US market — the Vietnamese tables contradict it at 5 stops.

Entry quotes: 250–260 triệu (Thanh Phát, Gia Đình, GHT); 400+ triệu for 5-storey (GemaLift). thangmaygde.com · thangmayght.com

Safety

Regulatory tie — both technologies must meet TCVN 6396-20:2017 (EN 81-20:2014) and periodic inspection (TT 36/2019 list + TT 12/2021 procedure). Failure modes differ: hydraulic = oil leaks, cylinder/pit corrosion; traction = ropes/counterweight wear. Both carry overspeed + UCM protection.

VDI 2225 recommends annual inspection for residential lifts. kone.co.uk · vsqi.gov.vn

Energy

Traction regen ahead. Schindler 3300 ships regenerative drive as standard; Otis Gen2 belt-driven. Hydraulic is catching up (VVVF + pressure accumulator, energy recovery, 2025 indirect-hydraulic improvements) but still trails.

Lifts use 2–10% of building energy (VDI 2225). Typical home lift ~300.000 đ/month electricity (vendor figure, unverified). VDI 2225 (2024) · Energies (2025)

Shaft fit (1000×1400)

Borderline for both. 1.4 m² sits at the lower bound of the mini segment (one installer cites ~1.5 m² practical minimum, 250–350 kg; GHT lists cars 600×830 to 1100×930). Fit is per-product — no public shaft drawings found for the big brands.

Hard data gap: per-product shaft/pit/overhead drawings must come from supplier quotes. gde · ght

Regulatory trap

EN 81-41 ≠ a real lift. EN 81-41:2024 / TCVN 6396-41:2018 cover only ≤0.15 m/s platform lifts for persons with impaired mobility (carrier not fully enclosed). A 5-stop enclosed-car lift must be designed to EN 81-20/50. Common market confusion — check the spec sheet, not the brochure.

EN 81-41:2024 scope · tns-lift TCVN guide
