# Brainstorm: Sell the SSR Apartment to Fund the Tubehouse Build

## Problem & Why Now

The tubehouse design (4m x 25m, 5 storeys, mixed-use) is fully specified in this repo across
`research/2026-07-06_tubehouse-dream-home-brainstorm.md`, the drawing pipeline, and the
contractor-as-drawn viewers. The design is done; the **capital is not released**. The 72m2
2-bedroom apartment at Saigon South Residences (Nguyen Huu Tho, Phuoc Kien, Nha Be) is the
designated funding instrument, and it is currently an idle asset — the owner already rents
elsewhere at 12tr/month near the build site.

Why now: HCMC secondary-market conditions in Q3 2026 are **divergent** — asking prices are at
record highs and firm, while transaction liquidity is falling (one source: secondary +2% QoQ;
another: secondary **down 2-6% QoQ with liquidity notably reduced**, driven by leveraged sellers
exiting). A wide bid-ask spread punishes sellers who anchor to listing tables and rewards sellers
who price to actual signed transactions. This window rewards a disciplined, evidence-led sale
process rather than a passive listing.

## Current vs Desired State

- **Current state:** 72m2 2BR/2WC at Saigon South Residences. **So hong in hand, no mortgage.**
  Empty and fully furnished, ready to show. Not listed. Foreign-ownership-quota status unknown.
  Land for the tubehouse is **already owned**; no contractor signed; no sale channel established.
  The repo holds the completed design and drawing set but nothing about disposal or financing.
- **Desired state:** apartment sold within ~60-90 days at >= 5.15 ty, netting ~4.95-5.20 ty after
  2% PIT and commission; proceeds ring-fenced; contractor signed only after funds clear; build
  begins with a >= 600tr untouched contingency reserve.
- **Key repo surfaces:** none — this is a financial/transactional workstream, not a code change.
  Adjacent context only: `research/2026-07-06_tubehouse-dream-home-brainstorm.md` (DEC-003 4x25m,
  DEC-004 5 storeys mixed-use, DEC-018 "no budget stated" — this brainstorm closes that gap),
  `plans/2026-07-06-tubehouse-dream-home-plan.md`, `contractor/`, `deliverables/`.

## Market Research Findings (as of 2026-08-29)

### Comparable asking prices — two tiers, and the gap matters

| Source tier | 71m2 | 75m2 | 76m2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aggregator "reference" tables (angialand, phuloiland, Aug 2026) | 5.0-5.1 ty | 5.2-5.5 ty | 5.6 ty |
| **Live dated listings** (housenow, 06-27 Aug 2026) | **5.65-5.95 ty** (79-84tr/m2) | **5.70-6.00 ty** (76-80tr/m2) | **5.50 ty** (72tr/m2) |

- Pro-rating to 72m2: **live ask band ~5.7-6.0 ty**; aggregator band ~5.05-5.25 ty.
- District 7 condo median list ~59.8tr/m2 (Dot Property); SSR trades **above** it on Phu My Hung
  developer branding despite the Nha Be address.
- **Noise warning:** cafeland and some portals show 71-75m2 units at 2.3-3.6 ty. These are almost
  certainly bait listings or HDMB remaining-payment quotes, not full transfer prices. Do not let a
  panel agent cite them as comps — the correction is that the So hong is issued and the unit
  transfers freehold.
- Rental band at SSR: 12-22tr/month, useful as a yield cross-check for investor buyers.

### Costs and drag

- **PIT on transfer: 2% of gross contract value, final** — not on gain, not reducible by
  documenting the purchase price. On 5.4 ty that is 108tr.
- **Commission: no legal cap in Vietnam.** Primary/project sales run 2-3%; **secondary resale
  apartments run 1-2%**, typically seller-paid. A 1% mandate gets shown after the 2% listings.
- **Days on market:** well-priced HCMC resale 60-100 days; overpriced or legally uncertain
  120-180+ days.

### Build side

- 500m2 GFA (100m2 footprint x 5 storeys). HCMC turnkey nha pho 2026: **5.0-9.5tr/m2**, with
  5.0-7.5tr/m2 the common band for a 5-storey; +10-15% per storey above ground for structure.
- Construction estimate: **3.0-3.75 ty**. Plus design/permits ~150-250tr, FF&E ~300tr, and rent
  during the build at 12tr x ~15 months = 180tr (already being paid; not incremental to the sale).
- **Reconciliation:** net proceeds 4.95-5.20 ty vs all-in build 3.7-4.4 ty => **buffer 0.6-1.5 ty.**
  The numbers close with genuine margin. This is not a squeeze.

## Resolved Decisions

- **DEC-001:** Sale funds **construction only**; the tubehouse land is already owned — the single
  fact that makes the whole plan viable.
- **DEC-002:** **No bridge-housing problem.** The owner already rents at 12tr/month near the build
  site and the apartment is empty. The sale can be immediate and unconditional; no leaseback.
- **DEC-003:** Optimize for **balanced: 60-90 days at a market-clearing price**, not maximum speed.
- **DEC-004:** **So hong in hand, no mortgage** — fastest legal path: notarized SPA, 2% PIT,
  transfer in weeks. This is a genuine marketing asset; lead with it.
- **DEC-005:** **Open panel of three agents from day one** (not exclusive). Matches the owner's
  stated preference and the original request.
- **DEC-006:** Panel discipline layer to neutralize the open-panel failure mode: **one fixed price
  across all three agents**, a **seller-owned photo/video/floorplan asset pack** issued identically
  to each, and a **first-registration attribution rule** (the agent who first registers a named
  buyer in writing owns that buyer for 30 days). Without this, three agents undercut each other on
  the same unit and none invests in marketing.
- **DEC-007:** Unit is **empty and fully furnished** — show-ready. Spend 15-25tr on paint touch-up,
  deep clean and professional photography. Furnished units anchor the top of the comp band; the
  cheap listings in the data are overwhelmingly bare shells.
- **DEC-008:** **Foreign-ownership quota status must be checked with the SSR management board
  before listing** — a single phone call. If quota-eligible, add English-language marketing
  (Hoozing, Rever EN, Realtique) and defend the 5.75-5.95 band; historically worth 5-12%.
- **DEC-009:** **Sell first, then sign the contractor.** Costs 2-3 months of build delay and
  eliminates the risk of owing progress payments against an unsold asset.
- **DEC-010:** **List 5.75 ty; walk-away floor 5.15 ty.** Anchored to live dated August listings
  (~80tr/m2), not aggregator reference tables. The ~10% negotiation room is deliberate: in a
  falling-liquidity market the discount to close runs **7-12% off ask, not the usual 3-5%**.
- **DEC-011:** **Commission 1.5% winner-takes-all, plus a 0.3% bonus if closed within 45 days.**
  Sits inside the 1-2% secondary-resale norm; worst case ~1.8% = ~97tr on 5.4 ty. Rejected 1% flat
  — below-market for an open panel and works directly against the speed goal.
- **DEC-012:** **Cash buyers preferred; 5% dat coc deposit with forfeiture; 30-day close.**
  Mortgage buyers accepted only with a bank pre-approval letter and a 45-day cap, because bank
  appraisal can come in under contract and reopen the negotiation at week 4.
- **DEC-013:** **No hard deadline** — the build starts when the sale closes. This preserves full
  pricing leverage and lets the floor actually be held.
- **DEC-014:** Agent selection is delivered as a **vetting protocol plus a candidate pool the owner
  runs himself**, not three named picks. Search visibility measures marketing budget, not closing
  ability; naming three from listing-site prominence would be false precision.

## Agent Panel: Vetting Protocol

Call 6-8 candidates, score each out of 100, take the top 3.

| Criterion | Weight | How to score |
|---|---|---|
| **Verified SSR closings, last 12 months** | 30 | Ask for unit numbers and closing months. A real agent names blocks and floors. Cross-check two against the management board. |
| **Written CMA quality** | 25 | Demand **signed transaction prices** for SSR in the last 90 days, not asking prices. An agent who submits portal asks as comps scores zero here — the single best filter in the current bid-ask spread. |
| **Response latency** | 15 | Time from first call to a written follow-up. Under 4 hours = full marks. Predicts how they will treat your buyers. |
| **Marketing commitment** | 15 | Will they pay for a boosted batdongsan.com.vn listing and a Nha Tot push? Get the spend in writing. |
| **Live buyer queue** | 10 | Do they have named buyers waiting for a 2BR at SSR *right now*? On-site brokers usually do; city-wide brands usually do not. |

## Assumptions & Constraints

- **ASM-001:** The unit is a standard 2BR/2WC ~72m2, mid-to-high floor, no defect or view penalty.
  A low floor or a poor outlook shifts the whole band down ~5%.
- **ASM-002:** Discount to close is **7-12% off ask** in current liquidity conditions. If offers
  cluster tighter than that, the list price was set too low.
- **ASM-003:** Build cost 6.0-7.5tr/m2 x 500m2. A premium finish specification pushes this 20-40%
  higher and would consume the entire buffer.
- **ASM-004:** 2% PIT applies as a final tax on gross contract value, with no gain-based relief.
- **CON-001:** The open-panel structure (DEC-005) means no agent has exclusivity leverage; the
  discipline layer in DEC-006 is **load-bearing**, not optional.
- **CON-002:** Liquidity is falling faster than prices are. Time on market is the real risk, not
  price erosion — but DEC-013 (no deadline) absorbs it.
- **CON-003:** Under-declaring the contract value to reduce the 2% PIT is a common local practice
  and is **out of scope** — it creates tax and enforcement exposure and is excluded by default.

## Approaches Considered

- **Chosen:** Sell outright via a disciplined 3-agent open panel at 5.75 ty, close within 60-90
  days, then fund the build from cleared proceeds. Highest certainty, no leverage, no financing
  cost, and the buffer survives a 20% build overrun.
- **ALT-001:** *Keep the apartment, mortgage it to fund the build.* Rejected — carries interest
  through a 15-month build with no offsetting income, and the Q3-2026 data shows leveraged holders
  are precisely the sellers being forced into discounts.
- **ALT-002:** *Rent the apartment out (12-22tr/month) and fund the build another way.* Rejected —
  the yield does not come close to 3.0-3.75 ty of construction, and a tenant in place cuts the
  future buyer pool to investors only.
- **ALT-003:** *Full 90-day exclusive at 2%.* Rejected by DEC-005. Would buy more agent effort and
  a single coherent price story, but risks burning the whole window on one wrong pick.
- **ALT-004:** *Price-first at 5.95 ty, accept 120-180 days.* Viable given no deadline, but the
  live 76m2 listing at 5.50 ty shows a larger unit already undercutting that ask — the market
  would route buyers around you.

## Out of Scope

- Tubehouse design changes — settled in the July brainstorm and the contractor drawing set.
- Contractor selection, tendering, and construction-phase management.
- Tax minimization via under-declared contract value (CON-003).
- Mortgage, bridge-loan, or any leveraged financing structure.
- Sale of any other asset.

## Open Questions

1. **Q-001:** Is the unit inside the building's 30% foreign-ownership quota?
   - **Recommended default:** Assume **not eligible** and price to the domestic band (5.75 list)
     until the management board confirms otherwise.
   - **Why this matters:** Eligibility unlocks a separate buyer pool and 5-12% of price. It is one
     phone call and should be **action item #1**, before any photography or listing.
2. **Q-002:** What floor, block and outlook is the unit? River view materially changes the band —
   the live 5.95 ty ask at 71m2 is explicitly a river-view unit.
   - **Recommended default:** Assume mid-floor, standard outlook; hold 5.75 list / 5.15 floor.
   - **Why this matters:** A high-floor river-view unit supports listing at 5.95 instead.
3. **Q-003:** What finish specification is assumed for the tubehouse — standard or premium?
   - **Recommended default:** Standard/mid finish at 6.5tr/m2 => ~3.25 ty construction.
   - **Why this matters:** A premium finish (+20-40%) pushes the build to ~4.5 ty and erases the
     contingency reserve, which would reopen DEC-009 and DEC-010.
4. **Q-004:** Should there be a scheduled price-reduction trigger if no offer arrives?
   - **Recommended default:** With no deadline (DEC-013), hold the price to day 90, then reassess
     against fresh CMAs rather than cutting on a timer.
   - **Why this matters:** Determines whether the plan needs automatic markdown gates.
