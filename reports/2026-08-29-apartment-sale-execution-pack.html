01 / Executive Summary

Executive Summary

The asset

72.0 m2, 2BR/2WC, river view, high floor at Saigon South Residences (Nguyen Huu Tho, Phuoc Kien, Nha Be). Sổ hồng issued, no mortgage, empty and fully furnished. Trades above the District 7 median of 59.8 tr/m2 on Phu My Hung branding.

The pricing

List 5.95 ty (82.6 tr/m2), target settlement 5.45–5.60 ty, published floor 5.35 ty. Anchored to the August 2026 live river-view comparable at 71 m2 / 5.95 ty / 83.8 tr/m2 — not to lagging aggregator tables.

The mechanism

Open panel of exactly three agents from day one, on three non-negotiable discipline rules: one fixed price, seller-owned photo/video/floorplan pack, and a 30-day first-registration buyer attribution rule. Winner-takes-all 1.5% + 0.3% speed bonus.

The funded conclusion

Medium-finish tubehouse (500 m2 at 6.5 tr/m2) costs 3.25 ty construction, 3.75 ty all-in, 4.075 ty with 10% contingency. At the 5.35 ty floor the sale nets 5.143 ty — the build is funded with 1.068 ty of headroom, 750 tr held as reserve.

04 / Agent Panel — How the Three Are Chosen

Agent Panel

An open panel of exactly three agents from day one — no exclusivity. Selection is by a 100-point scorecard after calling 6–8 candidates. The candidate pool is derived from search visibility and is unverified on closing performance; that is what the vetting is for.

CriterionWeightWhat a strong answer sounds like
Verified SSR closings, last 12 months30Names blocks/floors and months; offers two unit numbers cross-checkable with the management board.
Written CMA quality25Cites 3+ signed transaction prices, last 90 days, with date/area/ty/tr per m2 — not portal asking prices (asking = 0 on this criterion).
Response latency15Written follow-up within 4 hours.
Marketing commitment15Names a boosted batdongsan.com.vn product and a Nha Tot push with spend in writing.
Live buyer queue10Named buyer with budget/timeline for a 2BR at SSR right now.
Terms acceptance5Accepts 1.5% winner-takes-all + 0.3% bonus, fixed 5.95 ty, and the 30-day attribution rule without argument.

Three discipline rules (non-negotiable)

  1. One fixed listing price — 5.95 ty identical across all three agents.
  2. Seller-owned photo/video/floorplan pack — no agent shoots or publishes their own images.
  3. First-registration attribution — the agent who first registers a named buyer in writing owns that buyer for 30 calendar days.

Commission

1.5% of gross contract value, winner-takes-all, plus a 0.3% bonus if the notarised agreement is signed within 45 days of listing. Losing panel agents receive nothing. See 04-mandate-terms-sheet.md for the send-as-is letter.

This pool is derived from public search visibility, which measures marketing spend rather than closing ability. No claim is made about any firm's transaction history. Score them yourself using the rubric.

05 / Sale Timeline

Timeline — Query to Keys

There is no hard deadline. The build starts when the sale closes. Price is held to day 90, then reassessed against fresh signed-transaction CMAs. The schedule below is indicative — the gates are the control.

Pre-listing (week 0)

  • Confirm foreign-ownership quota with the SSR management board — one phone call; gates the English-language channel.
  • Spend 20 tr: paint touch-up (8 tr), deep clean (4 tr), photography + floorplan + video (8 tr).
  • Call 6–8 agents, score, appoint 3; issue the identical mandate terms sheet and collect signed acceptance.

Listing & viewings (weeks 1–6)

  • Go live on batdongsan.com.vn and nhatot.com (boosted placement per agent). Distribute the seller-owned asset pack identically.
  • First-registration log runs from day one. Every offer in writing.

Offer & deposit (weeks 4–10)

  • Negotiate against the ladder in 08-negotiation-ladder.md. Cash preferred; mortgage only with pre-approval letter.
  • 5% dat coc deposit with forfeiture on buyer default.

Close & fund (weeks 8–12)

  • Notarised sale-purchase agreement at a licensed notary; 2% transfer tax on gross; So hong transfer. Close in 30 days (cash) or 45 days (mortgage).
  • Waterfall via scripts/sale_waterfall.py; release 4.075 ty to the build in stages, hold 750 tr reserve.

07 / Closing & Tax

Closing Sequence

  1. Accepted offer in writing (buyer name, price in ty, deposit readiness, cash vs mortgage).
  2. Deposit contract (hợp đồng đặt cọc) — 5% dat coc, forfeiture on buyer default.
  3. Document assembly — So hong original, CCCD/passport, marriage or single-status certificate, household registration.
  4. Notarisation of the sale-purchase agreement at a licensed notary office.
  5. 2% transfer tax on gross contract value — 119 tr at 5.95 ty, 110 tr at 5.50 ty, 107 tr at 5.35 ty (final, not on gain, unaffected by original purchase price).
  6. Registration fee.
  7. Transfer of the So hong to the buyer's name.

Mortgage-financed buyers only with a bank pre-approval letter; close capped at 45 days. A bank valuation below the contract price can reopen the negotiation around week 4 — the specific reason cash buyers are preferred. See 09-closing-tax-checklist.md.
