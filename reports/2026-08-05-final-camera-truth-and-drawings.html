01 / Executive Summary

Executive Summary

What was done

Six phases across the homedesign pipeline, in the order that makes each one trustworthy: fix the cameras so renders show the building they name; upgrade the render engine (Blender 4.5 LTS + EEVEE Next) so a full gallery costs minutes, not an overnight run; add the physical detail an architect notices first (parapets, balustrades, ceilings, window sills, neighbour context); complete the drawing set with four elevations and two sections; make plot geometry honest and the warning channel truthful; and give every artifact a provenance hash so a stale brief can never ship silently.

The answer

Renders depict the building. The exterior camera was standing 4.69 m from a facade that needed 15.90 m (a one-character sign error in the analytic fit); every interior camera sat on the lawn outside its own room. Both are fixed and guarded by tests that provably fail on the old code. The 9-view final gallery renders in 50.7 minutes (EEVEE Next) versus 11.3 hours (Cycles). The architect brief grows from 17 to 23 pages and refuses to assemble from stale renders when asked. All 127 tests pass; lint and the skill-mirror gate are green.

02 / Background & Objective

Background & Objective

homedesign compiles a JSON home spec into a geometric model, then into 2D plans, a headless-Blender scene, renders and an A3 architect brief. After a prior sprint closed 88/88 items, the geometry was correct but the depiction was not: the exterior render cropped the building out of frame, every interior camera was placed outside the building, the framing regression test could not fail on either defect, the only validation warning that ever fired was unsatisfiable noise, and the shipped PDF embedded renders of a model state that no longer existed.

The objective of this plan was to fix depiction first, unlock affordable render quality by moving to Blender 4.5 LTS / EEVEE Next, then complete the drawing set an architect asks for after plans: elevations and sections.

  • DEC-002: the orphaned IFC exporter is deleted, not rewired.
  • DEC-003: backwards compatibility is mandatory for every spec.
  • DEC-004: textures/HDRI/asset libraries are deferred; geometric realism goes first.

Binding defaults & constraints

  • ASM-003: interior eye height 1.5 m, wall inset 0.35 m, lens 12–24 mm.

05 / Methodology

Methodology

The work was sequenced so each phase could be verified before the next was built on it. Camera math lives in pure Python (src/homedesign/ camera_fit.py) so it is unit-testable without Blender; the Blender builders became thin wrappers with no arithmetic left in them. A deliberate off-centre test was added that is mathematically invisible to the old symmetric-box suite — the teeth check proved it fails under the old sign and passes under the fix. A containment sweep asserts every interior camera stands strictly inside its room across every spec in the repo. The framing pixel test was tightened to require the building to clear all frame edges, and it fails on the old cropped render. Later phases relied on the same pattern: pure draw-model builders for elevations/sections, a byte-identity test for the compiler change, and model-hash sidecars so staleness is a machine-checkable fact rather than an assumption.

06 / Phase-by-Phase Analysis

Phase-by-Phase Analysis

PHASE-01 · Camera truth

One character fixed the exterior camera: fit_distance added the depth term when it must subtract it, so the camera pulled back for the far corners instead of the near ones. Interior cameras were rebuilt as inside-the-room placements with a solved focal length. The tightened framing test and the containment sweep are the permanent guards.

PHASE-02 · EEVEE Next

Blender 4.5.1 LTS installed alongside 4.1.1 and made the default; RENDER_PROFILES define preview / final / cycles; the final profile enables EEVEE Next raytracing and AgX. The 9-view gallery renders in 50.7 min against an 11.3-hour baseline.

PHASE-03 · Geometric realism

Parapets on balcony open edges, stair balustrades, top-storey ceilings, window lintels and sills, and neighbour party-wall massing plus a street strip — the elements whose absence made a correct model read as wrong.

PHASE-04 · Elevations & sections

A pure draw-model module projects four elevations (each side's own horizontal axis) and two sections (cut poché, slab bands, treads, room labels) into SVG and DXF. write_plans emits the complete 22-file set; the brief grows by six pages.

PHASE-05 · Honest geometry

site.wall_alignment: inside puts exterior walls' outer face on the plot line; Room.interior gives furniture, lights and cameras the net rect; wall_outside_plot becomes a real error. tubehouse-dream warnings drop 63 → 0; the north elevation is 4000 mm, not 4200.

PHASE-06 · Provenance & hygiene

Model-hash sidecars make stale renders detectable; --require-fresh makes them fatal. glTF + a self-contained offline viewer (three.js inlined, GLB embedded) ships without render time. Dead code removed, console script added, docs corrected.

07 / Findings & Recommendation

Findings & Recommendation

1. The sign error was real and one character long. The camera was placed at centre − dist·forward, so the binding corner is the nearest one and the depth term must be subtracted. Every existing test fitted a box centred on the passed centre, where the two signs return identical maxima — the defect was mathematically invisible until a deliberately off-centre test was added.

2. EEVEE Next is the right engine for this hardware. The 50.7-minute gallery is 13× faster than the 11.3-hour Cycles baseline; a single Cycles view of the smallest house still costs 9.8 minutes. EEVEE Next stays the final profile by binding default, and the raytracing flag is the recorded lever if a sub-30-minute gallery is ever required.

3. Honest geometry fixed the noise. The 63 permanent wall_outside_plot warnings were structurally unfalsifiable — exterior walls were centred on room edges and always poked 100 mm past the plot. Insets the walls to the plot line, made the rule alignment-aware and error-severity, and the channel carries signal again: tubehouse-dream now compiles with zero warnings, and its built width is truthfully 4.0 m.

4. Provenance closes the costliest failure mode. A brief that mixes current plans with six-week-old renders is now detected and refused (--require-fresh), because no artifact ships without the hash of the model it came from.

09 / Risks / Open Questions

Risks / Open Questions

Risks / caveats

  • Gallery above target: 50.7 min vs 30 min aspiration — recorded, binding default retained, lever identified.
  • Test thresholds recalibrated: the plan's framing-pixel numbers assumed no roof protrusion above the facade fit box and a wider facade; real thresholds differ but still fail on the old render.
  • EEVEE vs Cycles look: glass/opening rendering differs; previews validate layout, not lighting.
  • 28 MB of committed finals in deliverables/; regenerate-in-~50-min alternative exists.

Open questions

  • Is a wide-angle interior acceptable, or should interiors be cutaway exteriors (per-room wall suppression)?
  • Has the audience shifted from "a PDF I hand to an architect" toward the interactive GLB model — which should lead next?
  • Should homedesign gain a verify subcommand that re-checks freshness, camera containment and mesh-in-plot in one call?
