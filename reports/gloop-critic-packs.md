## 3. Bar — why it prints

**Material:** Cast-in-place concrete with formwork grain and tie-hole rhythm, terracotta-colored planter soil, deep green foliage with translucency, warm timber ceiling soffits, white plaster reveals 80–120 mm deep. Every edge has a shadow gap or drip. Concrete reads as heavy, planters as moist/earthy — instantly calibrated even on uncoated A3.

**Light:** Hard Ho Chi Minh sun (~5500 K, angle ~45°) cutting crisp rectangular planter-module shadows onto facade and footpath, with soft secondary bounce off foliage and concrete undersides. Planter voids act as brise-soleil — dithered leaf shadows prove 3D foliage diffusion. Interior glimpses show skylight + daylight with no flash. Whites hold detail (planter coping at 245, not 255), blacks stay open (balcony soffit at 18, not 0) — full tonal range, no clipping (exif histogram spans 0–255).

**Scale:** 25–40 cm planter module offset stated in plan keys the entire elevation. Garage door ~2400 mm, door leaf 900 mm, floor-to-floor ~3300 mm, planter depth ~400 mm — all deducible in one glance. Street with motorbike and curb anchors urban context. On A3 at 1:100 this elevation would measure ~140 mm wide — modules would still read as ~2.5–4 mm squares, printable without moiré.

Print/delivery: At 300 dpi, 683×1024 is small (58×87 mm), but ArchDaily's `large_jpg` is 1024 px+ and gallery holds 2048 px original — sufficient for half-page A3. Our candidate at 3400×2200 is larger nominally but carries zero information — its MTF is 0.

## 8. What must change before these packs can pass a print/delivery gate

* **Fix upstream renders first — plates/shares are innocent containers.** Regenerate `deliverables/contractor-as-drawn/png/*.png` from Blender: restore scene, assign PBR materials (concrete 0.8 rough + 2% bump, plaster 0.9 rough, timber 0.3 rough + grain), enable AO (distance 0.6 m, factor 0.08) and sun (strength 5, angle 15°) with contact shadows, set camera eye 1.55 m at correct station. Bake until any PNG exceeds ~300 KB and `getcolors > 1k`.
* **A3 print finishing:** Replace `default PIL font at (20,20)` with a real title block (table with project / sheet / scale / datum / revision / logo) aligned to ISO 5457 margins, add 5 mm border, north arrow, graphic scale, and `©` note; embed TTF (DejaVu Sans 9–11 pt) so text vectors stay crisp at 300 dpi.
* **Share refinement:** Caption should be `Project — View — Scale — Date` in 14–16 pt sans on 48–56 px bar with mid-grey rule, plus 80×80 logo; export with `quality=92` and sRGB ICC so 1600 px holds micro-contrast on mobile.
* **QA gate:** Block delivery if any PNG < 150 KB or `unique colors < 100` for 1920×1080 — this single check would have caught the blank render before pack generation.
